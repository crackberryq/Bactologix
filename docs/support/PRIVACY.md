# Bactologix Support — privacy notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Publisher: **Jimi Goldstein · Human Link**.

## Scope and data flow

This notice describes the Bactologix Support skills-only plugin. Its package contains instructions and documentation. It has no developer-operated server, telemetry endpoint, external account connection, payment system, or automatic diagnostic upload.

The publisher does not receive or separately store your chats or attached diagnostics through this plugin. You choose what to type or attach in ChatGPT/Codex. The host processes that content to answer your request under its own terms, privacy policy, and applicable account settings. This plugin cannot control the host's retention, training settings, or deletion behavior, and does not promise that uploaded data are anonymous.

When you use host browsing or other separately enabled tools to retrieve public documentation, those tools and their providers have their own data practices. This plugin does not grant additional access to GitHub accounts or local files.

## Diagnostic exports

In Bactologix releases containing Get support, the export is generated locally. It includes version and selected job information, with optional bounded log excerpts. Input files, reports, full configurations, job payloads, and environment variables are excluded by the export implementation. Redaction of paths, sample identifiers and common credential patterns is best effort. Review and edit the preview before sharing; unrecognized sensitive text may remain.

Downloading the snapshot does not send it to the publisher. Attaching it to a conversation sends it to the host. Local copies remain on your computer until you remove them.

## Voluntary support requests

If you deliberately post a GitHub issue, the contents and your GitHub identity are visible to maintainers and, for this public repository, to the public. Maintainers use such submissions to understand and address the reported issue. Avoid including confidential data. The plugin does not post issues automatically.

GitHub hosts support submissions and controls platform retention and account settings. Use GitHub's controls to edit/delete your own contributions where available, or request maintainer assistance through the support page without reposting sensitive details. Public copies outside the repository may not be removable by the publisher.

## Choices and contact

You can use documentation guidance without providing logs. You can omit log excerpts, remove details before sharing, delete local export files, and manage conversations/uploads through the host's available controls. For plugin privacy questions, see [support and contact](SUPPORT.md).

This notice covers this plugin's current implementation. Separate changes that add a backend, telemetry, live access, or additional data collection require an updated notice.
